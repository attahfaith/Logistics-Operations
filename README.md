     ### Results\findings

  The analysis results are summarized as follows:
1. Revenue is strong but the customer dependency is at risk
2. Fleet is operational but fuel costs are declining
3. The workforce is stable but idle time is a concern
4. Sales Growth shows a strong recovery at year 2021

### Recommendation

   Base on the Analysis, the Logistic Operations  is fundamentally healthy with a strong revenue base, stable workforce and active fleet. However three areas needs strategic attention which are;
- Reducing customer concentration risks
- Investigating the sharp fuel cost decline
- Addressing the high driver idle time to unlock greater operational efficiency and profitability
       
  ### Limitations

   The primary limitation of this analysis is that the dataset may not reflect in real time operational changes, as it cannot captured live fluctuations in fuel prices, drive avalability, customer contracts, and route conditions that directly impact business decisions.
